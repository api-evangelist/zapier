If your company's idea of "using AI" is keeping ChatGPT open in a browser tab—congrats, you're doing the bare minimum. You're also missing out on the real efficiency gains AI can deliver. Ad hoc prompting is great for one-off requests ("draft this email," "explain this bizarre spreadsheet formula"), but it doesn't scale—and it definitely doesn't count as integrating AI into your workflows.
