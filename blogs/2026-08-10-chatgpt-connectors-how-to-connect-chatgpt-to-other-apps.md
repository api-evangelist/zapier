ChatGPT was born, and named, to chat. It's scarily good at it, but if you're using it for real work, you want to actually do something from the chat window: update a record based on ChatGPT's research, send a message with the genius idea it beat you to the punch on, or just log the output somewhere so you don't lose it. There are a lot of ways to connect ChatGPT to other apps.
