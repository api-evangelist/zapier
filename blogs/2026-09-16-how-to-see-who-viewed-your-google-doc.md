If you send a Google Doc to a stakeholder and get crickets, it could mean one of two things: (1) they never opened it, or (2) they opened it but got sidetracked by something infinitely more engaging—like reading the entire terms and conditions of a software update. Instead of assuming they thought your ideas were trash and preemptively drafting your resignation (because what else would you do?), there's a way to actually see who's viewed your doc.
