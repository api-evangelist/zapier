Sometimes it feels like every time I blink, technology transports me into the future at blistering speeds, and it's all I can do to keep up. Then someone asks me to sign something on paper and I remember: we're not quite living in Star Trek yet. I once signed a lease in front of a notary (in triplicate, of course, initialing every page); DHL-ed it to my co-signer in another city to be signed and notarized again; then bundled everything together to submit it.
