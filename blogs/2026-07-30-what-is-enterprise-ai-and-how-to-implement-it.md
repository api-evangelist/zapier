There are two ways to bring AI into a large company. You can start with a specific problem and build out from there. (In my years as a tech writer interviewing enterprise leaders, that's the approach behind pretty much every success story I've covered.) Or you can buy a stack of AI licenses, roll them out company-wide, and hope for the best.
