If you're here, you're looking for one of two things: a workflow builder to help you ship automations faster, or an embedded SDK you can drop into your own product so users can connect their accounts without you building OAuth infrastructure from scratch. I work at Zapier, which solves both of these problems, and I'd unsurprisingly argue it's the best Pipedream alternative. But there are, of course, other automation tools and integration SDKs worth considering.
