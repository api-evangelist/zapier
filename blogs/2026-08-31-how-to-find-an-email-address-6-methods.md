I once spent more minutes than was reasonable looking for someone's email address. I checked their website, scanned their LinkedIn, searched their name with every Google operator combo I could think of, and eventually found myself scrolling through photos of a conference they'd attended and zooming in on the conference badge to see if their social handle was hiding in plain sight. It was not.
