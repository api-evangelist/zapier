Once you're in the Salesforce universe, it's hard to move out. Part of that is because it's getting the job done: it can do almost anything, it scales to almost any size, and its ecosystem is enormous. If you have a dedicated Salesforce admin, a development team, and a budget, it's the gold standard for CRM, by a long shot.
