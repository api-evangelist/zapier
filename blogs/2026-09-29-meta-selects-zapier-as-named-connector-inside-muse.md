Zapier today announced that its connector is now available in Meta's Muse agent. Once authorized, users can connect Muse to more than 9,000 apps and 40,000 actions through Zapier MCP. With Zapier MCP, Muse can take action across the tools your team already uses, but only the specific apps and actions you approve.
