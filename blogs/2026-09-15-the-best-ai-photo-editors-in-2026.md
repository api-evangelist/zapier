While AI image generators are still attracting most of the headlines, AI photo editors—apps that take your image and use AI to edit or improve it in some way—have been starting to generate some buzz. And really, AI photo editors are the more useful tool. Most of the time, you aren't looking for some wild image of a Canadian man riding a moose through a maple forest in the style of a long-dead impressionist artist.
