As a content creator at , I'm always looking for ways to produce more without sacrificing my voice. ChatGPT's custom instructions and the ability to build custom GPTs made it easy for me—once I figured out how to train it to actually sound like me. The gist is that you can use ChatGPT to analyze a few samples of your writing style, including voice, tone, and structure, and then paste those descriptors into ChatGPT's custom instructions.
