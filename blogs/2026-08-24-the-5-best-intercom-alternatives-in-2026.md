Intercom is an impressive platform, designed to handle the entire customer lifecycle, across every touchpoint, from onboarding through retention. But not every business needs that level of commitment from its help desk. If you're looking for something a little less complex or a little less eerily omniscient, there are plenty of Intercom alternatives for customer support, customer engagement, and customer experience.
