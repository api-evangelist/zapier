AI agents are everywhere right now, and platforms like Gumloop are betting that enterprises want tools built specifically to design, launch, and manage agents. But here's the question: do you need a specialized app for agentic workflows, or a platform that integrates agents more broadly into your existing business processes? Most enterprises already use dozens of tools across departments, including CRMs, project management software, HR platforms, and communication apps.
