From writing your resume for you to automating networking emails, there are tons of ways AI can help streamline your job search. But more importantly, AI can also help you cut through the noise, secure interviews, and impress hiring managers—as long as you use it well. According to a survey from Software Finder, 75% of job seekers use AI tools as part of their job search, with 14% using AI throughout the entire application process.
