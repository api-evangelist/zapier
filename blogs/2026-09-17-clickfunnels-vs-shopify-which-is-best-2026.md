Yoto, an audio player for kids, uses Shopify to power its 1,200-product eCommerce store. You can browse newly released products, bundle items together at a discount, and ship your purchase almost anywhere in the world. Alex Hormozi, an author and entrepreneur, used ClickFunnels to grow a coaching company called Gym Launch, which used single-offer funnels relying on lots of testimonials and proof points to make the sale.
