Quoting someone in Slack is either the clearest thing you can do in a conversation or a passive-aggressive power move, depending on who you ask. We're not here to relitigate that debate (it's the clearest thing you can do, obviously). We're here because Slack doesn't have a built-in quote feature the way most modern chat apps do—and you'd like to know what to do about that.
